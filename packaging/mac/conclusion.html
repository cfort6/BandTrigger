BandTrigger is installed

In Ableton Live:

  1. Open Settings → Plug-Ins, turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders, and click Rescan.
  2. On a MIDI track, load BandTrigger Instrument (under Plug-Ins → BandTrigger).
  3. Open the device's Sidechain section and pick your drum track.
  4. On a second MIDI track with your Drum Rack, set MIDI From to the BandTrigger track and choose BandTrigger Instrument below it. Set Monitor to In on both tracks.

The plug-ins are in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. To remove them, delete the two BandTrigger .vst3 files there (and the Applications/BandTrigger folder).

BandTrigger is installed

In Ableton Live:

  1. Open Settings → Plug-Ins, turn on Use VST3 Plug-In System Folders, and click Rescan.
  2. Put BandTrigger on your drum audio track.
  3. On a MIDI track with your Drum Rack, set MIDI From to the drum track and choose BandTrigger below it. Set Monitor to In.
  4. Pick a band along the top, then drag in the spectrum: left/right moves it, up/down widens or narrows it.

The plug-ins are in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins/VST3. To remove them, delete the two BandTrigger .vst3 files there (and the Applications/BandTrigger folder).

BandTrigger

Turns drum hits in an audio track into MIDI notes. It has 8 frequency bands, each sending its own note, so one instance can trigger a whole kit into a Drum Rack or sampler.

This installs:

  • BandTrigger: an audio effect for the drum track. Use this one in Ableton Live, Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase and others.
  • BandTrigger Instrument: an alternative that goes on a MIDI track and listens to the drums through its sidechain input.
  • Standalone apps, if you want to try it outside a DAW.

Click Customize on the Installation Type step to choose what gets installed.

BandTrigger

Turns drum hits in an audio track into MIDI notes. Pick a frequency band, and every hit in that band triggers a note you can send to a Drum Rack or sampler.

This installs:

  • BandTrigger: an audio effect for the drum track (Reaper, Bitwig, Cubase and others).
  • BandTrigger Instrument: the version for Ableton Live. It goes on a MIDI track and listens to the drums through its sidechain input.
  • Standalone apps, if you want to try it outside a DAW.

Click Customize on the Installation Type step to choose what gets installed.
